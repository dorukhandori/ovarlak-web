Ovarlak Yayinci

Connect our TikTok account

Ovarlak Yayinci is the internal tool that sends Ovarlak Gaming's own daily short video to our own TikTok account. It never posts by itself:

  1. We connect our TikTok account once with the button below (basic profile + upload as draft).
  2. Each day, after the video goes live on YouTube, our server uploads the same video to TikTok as a draft, with its caption.
  3. TikTok sends us an inbox notification; we open the draft in the TikTok app, edit it if we want, and post it ourselves.
