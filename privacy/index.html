Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the website ovarlak.games and Ovarlak Yayinci, a small internal tool that Ovarlak Games (also known as Ovarlak Gaming) uses to publish its own short gaming videos to its own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Ovarlak Yayinci is not offered to the public: the only accounts connected to it are Ovarlak's own.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok: the basic profile of our own TikTok account (account identifier, display name, profile picture) and permission to upload our finished videos to that account as drafts, which we then publish ourselves in the TikTok app.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): our own Facebook Page and the Instagram professional account linked to it, permission to publish our videos there, and the view, like and comment counts of our own posts. It also reads the public profile and public post counts of other Instagram professional accounts through Meta's Business Discovery, to learn which gaming topics work.
  • YouTube: our own channel, to upload and schedule our videos and read their public counts.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store or process any data about the people who watch, like, comment on or follow our videos. We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell or share it with anyone, and we do not connect anyone else's account to the tool.

Where it is kept

Access tokens and the identifiers, captions and counts of our own posts are kept in private files on our own server in Germany, readable only by the tool. They are kept for as long as the tool is in use and deleted when the connection is revoked.

Revoking access

The connection can be removed at any time: in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions; in Meta under Business integrations; in Google under Third-party connections. Revoking ends all access and we delete the related tokens.

The website

ovarlak.games is a static site. It does not use cookies or tracking, and it does not ask for personal data. Our games link to their own pages, which state their own data use.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: dev@ovarlak.games.
